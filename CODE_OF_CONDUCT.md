# Community conduct

Discuss the code and evidence. Respect people with different backgrounds and experience. Give clear, constructive feedback; avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, and publishing another person's private information.

Do not include personal files, credentials, or unsafe real-data reproduction steps in public reports. Use disposable fixtures and redact paths.

The repository owner may remove content or restrict participation that violates these expectations. Before opening the project to external contributors, the owner must publish a private conduct-reporting contact and moderation process. Security reports follow SECURITY.md. No contact address is invented here.
