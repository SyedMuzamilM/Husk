# Support

For reproducible bugs or feature requests, use [GitHub issues](https://github.com/SyedMuzamilM/Husk/issues) once the repository is public. Include the Husk revision, macOS version, Mac architecture, and a disposable reproduction. This volunteer project has no response-time guarantee.

For vulnerabilities or suspected wrong-file removal, email [muzamil@mzml.dev](mailto:muzamil@mzml.dev) privately. For conduct concerns, use the same address under the [code of conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md). Do not post credentials, conversation contents, personal paths, or destructive reproductions publicly.

Husk moves reviewed items to Trash. If you need to restore an item, stop further cleanup and inspect Finder's Trash; use Put Back when available. Settings can show the recorded Trash destination if local history was enabled. Husk does not promise automated restoration or recovery after Trash is emptied.

For incomplete scans, check exclusions and the displayed coverage issues. Full Disk Access is optional and does not override Husk's protected-path policy. Sizes are allocated-space estimates; moving an item to Trash generally does not immediately free storage.

Build instructions are in the [README](README.md), and known limits are in [the audit ledger](docs/AUDIT_RESOLUTION.md).
